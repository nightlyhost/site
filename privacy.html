Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 September 2026

The short version. Nightly reads the guest messages in your Airbnb hosting inbox so it can write replies for you to approve. To do that it sends the guest's message and the facts you told it about your place — and nothing else — to one AI company that writes the draft. It never learns your Airbnb password, never looks at any other tab or site, and never sells or shares anything with advertisers. One button deletes the lot.

What Nightly collects

Only what it needs to draft a reply. Every row below exists because some part of the product would not work without it.

What Nightly never collects

  • Your Airbnb password. Nightly works inside the Chrome you are already signed into. There is nowhere to type an Airbnb password and nothing that would accept one.
  • Any other tab, site, or your browsing history. The extension runs only on airbnb.com/hosting/messages pages. It cannot see anything else.
  • Your guests' contact details or payment information. Airbnb does not show them to you, and Nightly does not go looking.
  • Your calendar. Nightly could once store a calendar feed URL. Nothing read it, so we removed it. Stay dates come from the inbox rows your browser already displays.
  • Anything for advertising. No ad networks, no trackers inside the extension, no profiles sold or shared.

Who else sees your data

Three companies, each doing one job:

  • Anthropic writes the drafts. It receives the guest's message, your house facts, and the reply it produces. Anthropic states that inputs and outputs from its commercial API are not used to train its models by default. Your door code is never included.
  • Stripe handles payment. If you subscribe, Stripe collects your payment details directly — they never pass through Nightly.
  • Google handles sign-in. We receive your email address and name; Google receives nothing about your guests.

Nobody else. We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we have no advertising business to sell it to.

Limited use of Chrome Web Store data

Nightly's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • Data is used only to provide and improve the single purpose of drafting Airbnb guest replies for your approval.
  • Data is transferred only to the providers listed above, and only as needed to run that feature, comply with the law, or protect against fraud and abuse.
  • Data is never used or transferred to serve advertising, including personalised, re-targeted or interest-based advertising.
  • No human at Nightly reads your data, except where you have asked us to — for example when you report a problem — where the law requires it, or for a security investigation.

How long it is kept, and how to end it

Your data stays while your account does. Under Plan → Delete everything in the Nightly queue, or from the extension's settings, one confirmation erases your house facts, your door code, every draft, every scheduled message, your listings, your sign-in sessions and your entire audit log from our servers. If you have a subscription it is cancelled first, so deleting your account can never leave you being charged for it. The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone, and your Airbnb account is untouched.

Ordinary server logs — which record that a request happened and roughly from where, but not what was in it — are kept for up to 30 days.

Security

Everything travels over HTTPS. Your door code is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it is written down, and is decrypted only at the moment an arrival message is built. Sign-in tokens are stored as hashes. Every row in our database is tied to one account, and every query is filtered by the account that asked for it.

We are a small operation, and we would rather say that plainly than imply a security team we do not have. If you find a problem, write to hello@nightlyhost.com and a person will answer.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can see everything we hold about you — it is in the app — correct it, and delete it, without asking us, without waiting, and without being charged. If you are in California, the EU or the UK you have further rights under the CCPA/CPRA, the GDPR or the UK GDPR; write to us and we will honour them. We will not treat you differently for exercising any of them, because there is nothing to withhold: the delete button is the same one we would use.

Children

Nightly is for people who host on Airbnb, which requires you to be 18. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect anything from them.

Changes

If we change what we collect or who sees it, we will say so here with a new date and tell you inside the app before the change takes effect — not quietly, and not only in a changelog.
