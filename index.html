Most hosts try AI messaging once

Then a guest asks about a refund, the bot answers warmly and wrongly, and the feature gets switched off for good. It isn't that the writing is bad. It's that you find out what was said at the same time the guest does.

You read it first

Every draft waits in your queue until you tap. Turn a question type loose only once you've watched it get answered right a dozen times — Wi-Fi and parking first, maybe nothing else, ever.

A list it can't cross

Money, cancellations, door codes before a booking, links, phone numbers, anything legal. Blocked whatever the switches say — and blocked by reading the message itself, not by trusting the AI's own read of it.

Receipts, not vibes

Every message proposed, approved, sent or refused is written down with the reason. When a guest quotes something back at you, you can look it up.

What it never does on its own

Whatever switches you turn on, these always land in your queue — or are refused outright. It's not a setting; it's the product.

  • Offer, promise, or discuss a refund, discount, fee, or price
  • Cancel, or walk a guest through cancelling
  • Send a door or gate code to anyone without a confirmed booking
  • Send a link, a phone number, or suggest paying off Airbnb
  • Accept or decline a booking request
  • Change a price or block dates without showing you the diff
  • Reply to anything about damage, complaints, lawyers, or police
  • Invent a fact that isn't on your house page — it says you'll confirm instead
